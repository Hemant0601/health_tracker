# Health Tracker — Play Console "App content" answers

Fill these in **Play Console → Policy and programmes → App content**.

## Privacy policy
`https://hemant0601.github.io/health_tracker/privacy-policy.html` (source: `docs/privacy-policy.html`).

## App access
**All functionality is available without special access** (no login).

## Ads
**No.**

## Content rating (IARC)
- Category: **All other app types**.
- Violence, sexuality, language, drugs, gambling, horror: **No**.
- Users interact or share content with each other: **No**.
- Shares location: **No**. Digital purchases: **No**.
- Expected rating: **Everyone / 3+**.

## Target audience
- **18 and over** only (adults record readings for their family; the app is not designed
  for children to use).
- Appeals to children: **No**.

## Data safety
- **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? → No.**
  All data is stored only on the device; the app has no servers, analytics or ads.
  Play's definition: data processed only on the device and never sent off it is *not*
  "collected".
- Encryption in transit / deletion questions are skipped when nothing is collected.
- Note: Play may ask about the **INTERNET** permission (added by Expo's libraries). The app
  makes no network calls with user data; CSV export goes only where the user shares it.

## Health apps declaration
- Features: **Health tracking (vitals: blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate, SpO₂, weight,
  temperature)**, **Reminders**.
- Medical device / regulated? **No** — personal record-keeping only; the app and listing
  carry a "does not diagnose or treat" disclaimer (Settings → Privacy & disclaimer).
- Health Connect: **not used**.

## Other declarations
Government app: No · Financial features: None · News: No · COVID-19: No.
Permissions: notifications, run at startup (to keep reminders after a reboot), vibrate,
wake lock. Storage and overlay permissions were removed in 1.3.0. No sensitive-permission
declarations needed. Exact alarms: not used.
