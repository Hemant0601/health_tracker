## Full description (1381/4000)

```
Keep your family's health readings in one simple, private place.

Health Tracker lets you log blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate, oxygen (SpO₂), weight and temperature for yourself and everyone you look after — parents, children, partner. See each person's trend at a glance and never forget a check with daily reminders.

FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• Add family members with their relation, age and height
• Each person gets their own readings, history and trends
• Switch between members in one tap

SIX VITALS, DONE RIGHT
• Blood pressure (systolic, diastolic and pulse)
• Blood sugar — fasting, after meals or random
• Heart rate and oxygen (SpO₂)
• Weight and temperature
• Choose your units: °F or °C, kg or lb, mg/dL or mmol/L

SEE HOW THINGS ARE GOING
• Clear charts for every vital
• Readings marked as within or outside the usual range, as a general guide
• Add a note to any reading and edit it later

REMINDERS
• Daily reminders for BP checks, sugar tests or anything else

YOUR DATA, YOUR PHONE
• No sign-up, no account, no ads
• Everything stays on your phone — nothing is sent anywhere
• Export to CSV to share with your doctor or keep a backup, and import it back any time

Health Tracker is for personal record-keeping. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for advice from your doctor. In an emergency, get medical help straight away.
```
